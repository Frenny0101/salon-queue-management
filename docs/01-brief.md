# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
Customers wait at a salon without knowing when their turn will come. Staff need a clear first-come-first-served queue that respects barber preferences and avoids duplicate active spots.

## Users
Customers, receptionist/owner, barbers.

## Must-have features
1. Join queue from a phone with customer name, 10-digit phone, up to three services and barber preference.
2. Show token, queue position, estimated waiting time and called status.
3. Reception dashboard: add walk-ins, call next, seat called customers, mark no-show and cancel.
4. Barber view: current customer, Start / Done and Available / On break.
5. Waiting-area TV: current customer by barber and next five tokens.
6. Owner settings: services (duration + price), barbers on duty and online queue open/close.
7. Avoid duplicate active queue entries for the same phone number.

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp, loyalty, multiple branches.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal `#1F2937` + gold `#D4A017`, clean, mobile-first. The reference guide requests English and Gujarati labels.
